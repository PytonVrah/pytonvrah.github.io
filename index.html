About me

I study Digital Game Theory at the University of Ss. Cyril and Methodius in Trnava. I make games from mechanics and art to release, both on my own and in a team.

I'm part of the game collective Chamraď, helped organise the FNURIK game festival in Trnava, and ran the student cinema Kino Ucho at my university for two years. I know what it takes to bring people together around games and films.

I worked at a VR arcade in Bratislava, helping customers and handling tech support, and in hospitality I learned to communicate under pressure. As a freelancer I take on game and art projects for external clients.

Community management appeals to me because I want to be there when players discover a game and bring their feedback back to the team.
